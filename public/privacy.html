Privacy policy

Chess Studio is an independent chess learning app published by leglerisaac. You can use its lessons, openings, drills, and local PGN review without an account.

Last updated September 29, 2026 · Applies to the Android app and the GitHub Pages website

What stays on your device

The app saves your chosen openings and imported repertoire, lesson completion, practice history, saved puzzles, and preferences in local app or browser storage. If you choose to analyze a public chess account, it also caches the username, recent public game data and reports. Imported PGNs and up to three full game reviews may be saved locally. Stockfish runs on your device to analyze positions; we do not send your PGNs, engine positions, or study history to a Chess Studio server.

You choose whether to import a PGN or backup file and where to save exports. Exported files remain in the location you select and are outside the app's local data deletion.

When a network request happens

When you enter a Chess.com or Lichess username and start account analysis, the app requests public games directly from that platform over HTTPS. The selected username is included in requests to that platform, which also receives ordinary network information such as your IP address. Chess.com requests may use an isolated iframe for its public API fallback. Chess Studio does not run its own account analysis server and does not require you to sign in to either platform.

Opening original game links or source and contact links sends you to the named external site, which handles the visit under its own policy. The GitHub Pages website is delivered by GitHub; GitHub may process routine access and security logs for the website. The Android app includes its learning assets locally and connects to external services only for features you choose to use.

See Chess.com's policy, Lichess's policy, and GitHub's policy for those services' practices.

How data is used and protected

Local study data is used to show your learning progress, game insights, and review schedule on this device. Public account requests are used only to generate the report you requested. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, sell your data, or create a Chess Studio account. The Android app stores its study data in app storage and disables Android backup. External API requests use HTTPS; local engine analysis works without an internet connection.

Retention and deletion

Locally saved study data stays on your device until you remove it. In the app or website, open Progress → Import, export, and reset → Delete all local data to erase the app's study keys on this device. You can also clear the website's site data in your browser or clear the Android app's storage in device settings; uninstalling the Android app removes its app storage. Deletion does not remove files you exported elsewhere or public game data maintained by Chess.com or Lichess. The JSON progress backup does not include cached account reports or full game reviews, so export anything you need before deleting.

Questions and changes

For privacy inquiries, open a GitHub issue for Chess Studio. Issues are public; do not include sensitive personal information. We will update this page and its date when our practices change.
